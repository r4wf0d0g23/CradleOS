# CradleOS UI

Raw’s standing direction, October 9, 2026: **simple information, nothing else.**

- Show concise labels, values, status and actions.
- Do not narrate implementation, data pipelines, audits, history or limitations in product chrome.
- Do not replace removed paragraphs with equally verbose tooltips or collapsed panels.
- Use a short qualifier where it changes the meaning: “Testnet”, “Historical”, “Unverified”, “Unavailable”, a time window, or a source/date.
- At irreversible actions, state the consequence briefly and keep the confirmation.
- Preserve real content: articles, lore, item descriptions, user-authored messages and decision-useful game rules/paytables.
- Keep detailed reasoning and provenance in repository documentation/release receipts.
- Presentation changes must not alter balances, permissions, safety gates or telemetry semantics.
