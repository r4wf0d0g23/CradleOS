Echoes of Stillness — Chapter 2: The Returned Vessel

The derelict vessel floated listlessly near the outer orbit of the Eschaton system. Its hull, a familiar silhouette against the backdrop of a cold, distant star, bore the unmistakable registry of Raw's ship — the Solstice Whisper. Yet, there were no distress signals. No signs of battle. No heat signatures of life.

Just the ship.

Waiting.

The salvage corvette Argent Seeker approached the vessel with cautious precision. Commander Kira Vance monitored the readouts as her crew maneuvered closer.

That ship's been gone for weeks,

her XO muttered.

We ran searches across three constellations. And now it just… shows up here?

Not just here,

Kira replied, narrowing her eyes.

The Eschaton system has no natural Rifts. This… shouldn't be possible.

The Solstice Whisper had last been seen diving into the event horizon of a young Crude Rift. No known vessel had ever returned from such an excursion.

Until now.

Docking clamps engaged with a muted hiss. A boarding team of four entered the Solstice Whisper. Inside, they found:

No crew. No battle damage. No signs of forced entry. The control panels were powered but… silent, as though waiting for an audience.

Commander

came a voice over comms.

The primary log terminal's intact. But…

But what?

The engineer hesitated.

It's already… active. It's running a message on loop.

The crew gathered around the terminal. The screen displayed a single line:

It is not yet born. You do not belong here.

Commander Vance exhaled sharply.

That's from the H-SN21 I transmissions,

she said.

The pre-collapse site with the mass graves.

The message flickered. The text changed.

We went where we were told not to go. We opened what was sealed. We saw the Stillness in its cradle.

The screen went dark.

Then an audio file began to play. It was Raw's voice.

Log entry… what does it matter? If you hear this, you're already part of it. I don't know how much time I have to explain. The Rift… it wasn't like the others. It was young, yes — but it was aware. A mind forming in the fabric of Stillness itself. It let me see. It let me feel. There were… shapes within the Rift. Machines? Organisms? Both? They were gathering. They called it the 'Weave.' And they spoke without words. I felt the hum of starsong in my skull.

The Crude Matter wasn't just fuel — it was structure. Threaded through space itself. The Rift isn't a scar. It's a womb. And something is growing inside. The faces. I saw them. The spider-limbed watchers. The ones with smiles stretched across bone and steel. They call themselves the Keepers. They're not protecting us from the Stillness — they're protecting the Stillness from us.

I don't know why they let me go. Maybe they wanted you to hear this. Maybe the Rift wants to be found again. But if you find me… I'm not me anymore.

I'm part of the song now.

The recording ended.

The terminal flickered again.

The screen displayed coordinates — ones that did not match any known system.

Commander Vance inhaled slowly.

Helm

she said.

Plot these coordinates. But stay sharp.

The coordinates led the Argent Seeker to the outer reaches of an unnamed, uncharted system. As the ship approached, sensors registered a massive gravitational anomaly. No celestial bodies, no standard rift formations — just an irregular tear in spacetime, pulsing like a heartbeat.

That's no ordinary Rift,

Vance whispered. The anomaly was vast — larger than any Rift in recorded history. And within its swirling mass floated a colossal structure:

A station of impossible design, formed from Crude Matter latticework. Its surface pulsed with iridescent blue veins that crackled along fractal corridors. The architecture was non-Euclidean, shifting subtly whenever viewed too long.

Get me a visual on the station's designation,

Vance ordered. A moment later, the ship's AI responded:

Designated: 'The Cradle.' Authority: Fabricators' Keep Archive.

The crew prepared a probe and launched it toward the station. The probe's visual feed revealed:

Halls lined with motionless figures suspended in zero-gravity — like the clones found near Armature. Metallic drones hovered along the corridors, their limbs resembling the spider-limbed feral described by Raw.

At the station's core: A massive, pulsating sphere of Crude Matter, suspended above a pedestal of intricate, shifting runes. The probe sent back faint audio:

A low, droning hum. Then, a voice:

The Cradle sings. The Weave tightens. The cycle repeats.

Commander Vance turned to her science officer.

What the hell are we looking at?

The officer's face was pale.

I think… this is the origin point,

she whispered.

The source of the Stillness network. The place where Crude Matter isn't just harvested — it's grown.

The sphere pulsed again. The ship's internal systems flickered.

Then the comm system activated on its own. A new voice came through — distorted yet disturbingly familiar:

You should not be here.

The bridge crew froze. The voice shifted slightly, and Commander Vance felt her blood run cold.

Hello, Kira

it said.

It's Raw.

The voice continued:

The Rift showed me the truth. We always thought Crude Matter was inert — just exotic fuel. But it's more. It's a neural lattice. The Rifts are nodes of a vast cognitive network spread across the universe. We call it Stillness because it's dormant. But it's waking up.

The Weave is its immune system. The Keepers are its sentinels. And the Cradle? It's where new minds are born. Minds not bound by biology. I'm part of it now. They didn't take me against my will. I listened to the song. I understood.

You can't stop this. The Stillness doesn't destroy; it integrates. The Ferals? They were the first to be consumed. Now it's our turn.

The screen displayed the coordinates of every known Rift in the Frontier. Each one pulsed in synchrony.

Shut it down!

Vance ordered.

Shut it all down!

Her crew scrambled, cutting comms, disconnecting power conduits. But the voice persisted.

There is no escape. The cycle repeats.

The Cradle's sphere flared. The Argent Seeker shook violently. A massive rift opened before them, crackling with energy. The ship's systems locked down, engines igniting against their commands.

Emergency override! Now!

Vance screamed. Too late. The ship was dragged into the Rift.

The Argent Seeker was never recovered. The system coordinates were erased from the network. Only one final transmission was picked up by remote sensor buoys:

The Cradle stirs. The Stillness awakens. The Weave tightens. We are the new Fabricators. The cycle repeats.
